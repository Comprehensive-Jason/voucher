# Loosening waits for the Morning boundary

Every Loosening, including changes to the Curfew and removing an Enforcer's protections, waits until the next Morning boundary (06:00); Tightening is immediate. Freedom's Locked Mode only protects sessions that are already running, so a 21:00 edit could cancel the 22:00 to 06:00 block. A fixed morning time, rather than a rolling 24 hour delay, means a Loosening requested at night never lands that night, and one requested during the day lands the next morning. Modelled on Pluckeye's delay, where changes to the delay are themselves delayed.
